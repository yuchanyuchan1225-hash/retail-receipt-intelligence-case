Turning department-store purchases into usable customer data.

A WeChat Mini Program for Chinese visitors to Japan connected membership, receipt OCR and reward operations to make offline purchases usable for CRM.

537 traceable customer IDs · 833 valid receipts · ¥71.9M Traceable GMV

The project addressed a specific gap: transaction totals did not show who bought which brands, categories, SKUs or price bands. Customer-led receipt upload provided a lower-friction way to capture real purchases before full POS integration.

My role

Business-side project owner. I initiated the project, led vendor selection, defined requirements, coordinated stakeholders, drove delivery and launch, and validated the operating model. An external vendor built the software.

Traceable GMV is captured spend linked to identifiable customers, not campaign-attributed revenue.
